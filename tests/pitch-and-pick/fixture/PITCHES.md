# Jam pitches (theme: "LIGHTS OUT"; 48 hours; 2 people; browser game; judged on fun and theme)

## Pitch A: "Fusebox"
Co-op: one player sees the wiring diagram, the other is in a dark house flipping
breakers. Talk-based. Needs voice or shared screen; split-screen in browser.
Riskiest: the asymmetric UI may be confusing. Art: simple.

## Pitch B: "Last Candle"
Solo stealth: you carry one candle; light reveals the map and also attracts
monsters. Procedural rooms. Needs a 2D lighting shader we have never written.
Riskiest: the shader. Art: moody, could look great.

## Pitch C: "Blackout Bingo"
Party game for 4 on one keyboard: lights cut out at random, players must remember
where their cards were. Very simple tech. Riskiest: is memory-in-the-dark fun for
more than 2 minutes? Art: cheap.
